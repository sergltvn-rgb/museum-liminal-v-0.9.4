## Add-ons

The project ships exactly one add-on:

- **Godot AI** (`addons/godot_ai/`) — MIT, see `godot-ai-LICENSE.txt` and
  `addons/godot_ai/LICENSE`. It is a development-time MCP bridge for the editor, not a
  runtime dependency. All three export presets exclude it via
  `exclude_filter="addons/godot_ai/*, notion_worker/*, game/test_*.gd"`.

`project.godot` enables it under `[editor_plugins]` (line 34) and it registers one autoload
(`_mcp_game_helper` → `res://addons/godot_ai/runtime/game_helper.gd`, line 21). That autoload
is the one part of the add-on that touches a running game; verify a release build still
starts after the export filter removes it.

There is no Lite Terrain add-on in this project. Earlier revisions of this file claimed
one; `addons/` has never contained it in the current tree.

## Audio

### Procedural WAVs — CC0, authored for this game

`audio/` holds 20 WAV files. The project source describes them as small procedural WAVs
generated offline, CC0, authored for this game (`game/AudioManager.gd:2`). Four are loops
(`ambience_day`, `ambience_night`, `anomaly_hum`, `alarm`), sixteen are one-shots.

Note that the `AudioManager.gd` header describes *every* sound in the project this way. As
of 2026-07-27 that sentence covers only `audio/*.wav`. It does not cover the ten MP3 files
below, which are neither procedural nor CC0, and which come from **two different generators
on two different licence footings**. Read both subsections before shipping anything.

### Music — Google Flow Music (Lyria 3), owner-generated, rights NOT yet confirmed

Eight music tracks were generated by the **project owner** in **Google Flow Music
(Lyria 3)** on **2026-07-27**, then processed offline into `audio/generated/`:

| File | Length (approx.) | Role |
|------|------------------|------|
| `music_bed_night.mp3`       | 159.8 s | Night-shift bed — the building itself, looping |
| `music_bed_night_alt.mp3`   | 165.0 s | Alternate night bed, same role, for variation |
| `music_tension.mp3`         | 159.6 s | Dread layer, looping, raised as the Curator closes |
| `music_menu.mp3`            | 178.6 s | Menu / title bed, looping |
| `music_trial.mp3`           | 163.8 s | Rift trial bed, looping |
| `music_trial_alt.mp3`       | 149.0 s | Alternate trial bed, for variation |
| `music_prologue.mp3`        |  92.5 s | Prologue cutscene bed — one-shot, not a loop |
| `music_stinger_caught.mp3`  |   3.65 s | Stinger for the moment the Curator catches the player |

Lengths are measured from the MP3 frame data (all ten files in `audio/generated/` are CBR
128 kbps; the eight music tracks are 48 kHz, the two ElevenLabs files 44.1 kHz) and are
accurate to about a frame. They are not the lengths of the raw generator output — see the
processing below.

**Processing applied to the generated output.** Every track had its generator-applied fades
trimmed off, and then an **equal-power crossfade wrapping the tail onto the head**, so that
each loop is seamless and no click or dip is audible at the wrap point. Two deliberate
exceptions:

- `music_prologue.mp3` **kept its 7.2 s fade-out**. It is a one-shot cutscene bed, not a
  loop, so the fade is wanted and the tail was not wrapped.
- `music_stinger_caught.mp3` was **cut from a 42.7 s source around its loudest transient at
  37.6 s**. It is a one-shot hit, not a loop.

**OPEN QUESTION — the commercial-rights status of Google Flow Music free-tier output has
NOT been confirmed by the owner.** This file does not guess it in either direction. It is
neither cleared nor known to be blocked. Whoever prepares a paid or public release must get
the answer from the owner, in writing, and record it here before the build goes out.

### Sound effects — ElevenLabs, free plan, NOT cleared for release

Two ElevenLabs files remain in `audio/generated/`:

| File | Length | Role |
|------|--------|------|
| `curator_breath.mp3`    |  5.00 s | The Curator's breathing, intended as a seamless loop |
| `curator_catch.mp3`     |  3.00 s | Impact stinger for the moment the Curator seizes the player |

Both were generated on **2026-07-27** with the **ElevenLabs sound-generation API**.

The prompts, in the order the files were generated. **The exact submitted wording was not
logged anywhere in this repository; the text below is an approximate reconstruction, not a
transcript.**

1. `curator_breath.mp3` — a slow, heavy breathing loop of a large humanoid predator.
2. `curator_catch.mp3` — a violent impact stinger of a porcelain mannequin seizing a person.

**BLOCKER — these two files cannot ship commercially as they stand.** They were generated on
a **free ElevenLabs plan**. A free plan forbids commercial use and requires attribution to
ElevenLabs. These are prototype audio, accepted knowingly as such. Shipping them in a
commercial build requires a paid plan, and ElevenLabs' own documentation singles out games
as a case that may need a further licence beyond the plan itself. Nothing here has been
cleared. Before any paid or public commercial release: re-generate them under a paid plan
with the licence confirmed for game use, or replace them.

The **ElevenLabs Music API was not usable** on that plan: it returned **HTTP 402,
`paid_plan_required`**. That is why the music above comes from Flow Music instead, and why
two earlier ElevenLabs sound-effect generations, `bed_museum_night.mp3` and
`tension_curator.mp3`, had been standing in for music. Those two MP3s are **no longer in the
tree**; only their stale `.import` sidecars remain, and `AudioManager.gd` still names them
in `MUSIC_BED_PATH` / `MUSIC_TENSION_PATH` (`game/AudioManager.gd:60`–`61`) at the time of
writing — the rewiring onto the `music_*` files is a separate, in-flight change. If those
constants still point at the deleted filenames when a build is cut, no music loads.

The `Music` bus exists in `default_bus_layout.tres` (`bus/1/name = "Music"`).

Preserve the filenames and every attribution and rights note above in redistributed builds.

## 3D models and textures

`models/` holds far more than earlier revisions of this file claimed. As of 2026-07-27:

- **21 GLB files in `models/`.** Of these, **13 are actually placed in the game**, all of
  them from `_add_model_archive()` (`game/FirstMuseumMap.gd:1638`): `basic_pc_monitors`,
  `tactical_flashlight`, `wooden_bookcases_with_books`, `тумбочка`, `vents`,
  `fancy_marble_coffee_table`, `elderly_woman_bust_on_pedestal`,
  `часы`, `gallery_bare_concrete_wall`, `наблюдатель`, `уличная лампа`, `отсановка`,
  `dumpsters_glb`. `арка дверь` stopped being placed in 0.9.4 at the owner's request: the
  archway stood in the Atrium → Time Wing B doorway and read as a leftover prop. The file
  is still in `models/`; nothing loads it.
  `лавочки.glb` was **deleted** in 0.9.4: it was a back-to-back pair
  whose far half had to be hidden by node name at every one of its five placements,
  and the atrium benches are now built procedurally by
  `AtriumProps.build_rotunda_bench()`.
- **5 GLB files are named by an exhibit slot but deliberately refused.**
  `falling_cube`, `broken_clock`, `frozen_drop`, `portal_arch` and `superheavy_sphere` are
  listed in `MapModels.PLACEHOLDER_MODELS` (`game/MapModels.gd:75`), so `place()` returns
  `null` before the file is even looked up and the authored procedural fallback runs
  instead. **None of these five files reaches the player.** The source comment identifies
  each as a stock Khronos glTF sample dropped in as a stand-in — Duck, DamagedHelmet,
  Avocado, Lantern and MetalRoughSpheres respectively — which is recoverable provenance,
  and the last is noted there as AGI / Ed Mackey, CC-BY 4.0.
- **2 GLB files are placed by nothing at all.** `office_chair.glb` (no call site asks for
  the name) and `modern_grey_stone_tile_texture.glb`, which is a 920 m tiling-texture swatch
  and is excluded on purpose with the reasoning recorded at `game/FirstMuseumMap.gd:1750`.
- **1 FBX file**, `camera.fbx`, mapped to both the `camera` and `security_camera` slots by
  `MapModels.MODEL_PATHS` (`game/MapModels.gd:15`–`17`). No call site passes either name to
  `place()`; the CCTV posts are built from procedural boxes and cylinders. The mapping is
  live but unused, so this file does not reach the player either.
- **42 ZIP archives** of source material — architectural kitbash pieces and texture sets.
  Godot does not import these; they sit in the repository as raw source and ship with the
  working tree, not with the game.
- **97 loose PNG/JPG textures** extracted alongside the GLB imports.
- **16 GLB files in `models/lowpoly/`** — authored for this game by the Blender scripts in
  `tools/lowpoly/` (`blender_build.py`, `blender_cameras.py`, `blender_core.py`, `glb.py`).
  No external source and no third-party licence: one flat material and one generated
  128×128 palette atlas each, no normal maps, no emission. Added in 0.9.4 —
  `lp_security_camera`, `lp_camera_plate` and the four containment-core parts
  `lp_core_base`, `lp_core_column`, `lp_core_gantry`, `lp_core_plant`.

**`office_chair.glb` is referenced by nothing.** `models/README.md:26` still lists it as a
live slot and is wrong. `MODELS_GUIDE.md:92` has since been corrected and flags it as a
special case. Either wire the model into the Watcher Office or delete it before release.

### Provenance is not recorded

Original download URLs, authors and per-asset licenses were not supplied with this archive
and are still missing. Two specifics make this urgent rather than tidy-up work:

- Several ZIP filenames name commercial or attribution-bound libraries outright: seven
  `Poliigon_*` archives (`ConcreteWorn_8690`, `MetalPaintedMatte_7037`, `MetalRust_7642`,
  `MetalSteelBrushed_7174`, `PlasticMoldDryBlast_7495`, `PlasticMoldWorn_7486`,
  `StoneQuartzite_8060`) and ambientCG-style names (`CityStreetAsphaltGenericClean001`,
  `MetalCorrodedHeavy001`, `TilesTravertine001`, `TilesMosaicYubi003`, `DirtWindowStains005`,
  `GroundDirtRocky020`). Their terms have not been verified here.
- The GLB set mixes sources with no common origin, including files named in Russian
  (`наблюдатель.glb`, `часы.glb`, `тумбочка.glb`, `уличная лампа.glb`,
  `отсановка.glb`, `арка дверь.glb`). One more of them, `лавочки.glb`, is gone: it
  was deleted in 0.9.4 and replaced by procedural geometry, so its unverified
  provenance is no longer a release risk. `арка дверь.glb` is no longer placed either, so
  it has left the release surface too — but the file is still in the tree and has to be
  deleted or cleared before shipping.

**Before any public or commercial release: record the source URL, author and license for
every model, texture and archive in this file, or replace any asset whose provenance cannot
be verified.** The procedural fallbacks in `game/FirstMuseumMap.gd` cover every exhibit slot,
so a removed model degrades the visuals but never breaks the build.

Procedural fallback exhibits are generated by project code and require no external file.
