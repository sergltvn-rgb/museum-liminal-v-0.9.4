The files in `audio/` are described by the project source as small offline-generated CC0 sounds. Preserve their filenames and this attribution note in redistributed builds.

## 3D models

The project currently ships six GLB assets: Broken Clock, Falling Cube, Frozen Drop, Office Chair, Portal Arch and Superheavy Sphere. The incorrectly named oversized fox asset previously stored as Newton Statue was removed; that exhibit now uses the project-owned procedural fallback. Their original download URLs and per-asset license records were not present in the supplied archive. **Before public or commercial release, add the source URL, author and license for every GLB here or replace any asset whose provenance cannot be verified.**

Procedural fallback exhibits are generated by project code and require no external model file.
